The “300%” rule: present 100% of the products, to 100% of your clients, 100% of the time. Preferred → Standard → Budget. Present every product before asking them to choose.
🤫

Stop talking

After you present the payment — wait. Let your client respond first. Silence closes more deals than another sentence.

❓

Consult the “No”

Any form of “no” or resistance is an invitation to ask a question. Responding with a question is attentive; responding with a pitch is pushy.

🎓

Be an expert

Know your repair costs, your labor rates, and your coverage terms. Discuss the client's concern before selling the benefit.

The C.A.R.S. method

Control the objection cycle every time:

  • Clarify / Isolate the concern
  • Acknowledge it genuinely
  • Respond with a question, not a pitch
  • Solution — present the path forward
