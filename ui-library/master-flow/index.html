Home — Deals Queue & Navigation7 screens

The portal opens on the queue, which is role-aware (2026-08-23): the Advisor gets a guided list (no filter pills, a Next line per deal), the Team Lead the original two-lane floor view (pills + archive). Both roles see the four identifiers on every row — name, VIN, stock, stage — with a chevron as the tap cue. There is no login: the app-bar role switch stands in for authentication, and the drawer holds global navigation and the demo reset.
