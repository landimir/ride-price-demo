Playbook · Section 3

The Menu

Finance, lease, and cash menus — where value gets built. Take control, present every product, and let the client choose.

Finance Menu · Step 1

Disclosure — every number, every time

The menu flow: ① Purchase Terms → ② Repayment Options → ③ Disclosure Forms → ④ Financial Contracts.

  1. Explain each number in detail and receive confirmation from your client. Once every individual number has been discussed, click Agreed Payment.
  2. Present the Agreed Payment.
  3. Present the Qualified Payment.
  4. Click Disclosure, set the frame, and proceed to Next.
Word track — agreed payment
“Earlier you agreed to a payment of $692.41 for 60 months at 3.5%, correct?”
Word track — qualified payment
“Great news — you qualified for 2.49% for 60 months, correcting your payment to $675.05. And that will buy the car today. However…”
Word track — the disclosure pivot
“Based on our conversations, that may or may not be the best way to own your vehicle. We will review your options and choose which you prefer.”
Finance Menu · Step 2

Taking control of the menu

On the repayment options page, immediately click Preferred — this is where you present the term, rate, and finance products.

Why click Preferred right away?

If you stay on the overview, your client naturally gravitates to the multiple payments being shown. Build value in the benefits before showing payments.

Present, then pause

With Preferred open, present every product without attempting to close after each one. Ask which option they choose only after presenting Preferred, Standard, and Budget. Pause and gain confirmation before proceeding.

Present 100% of the products · to 100% of your clients · 100% of the time The “300%” rule
Product Presentation

The Vehicle Service Contract walkthrough

Remind the client of the manufacturer's warranty and its exclusions, then move the mileage slider to reveal their real coverage window.

Word track — reframing factory coverage
“Your manufacturer provides a comprehensive coverage, sometimes referred to as your bumper to bumper warranty, for 3 years and 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. This will cover the mechanical components, safety features and technology in your vehicle. The warranty from the manufacturer does not include items such as accident/total loss, wear and tear, maintenance, road hazard, or the appearance.”
Word track — their actual coverage window
“You'd indicated that you drive approximately 15,000 miles per year, correct? Based on your driving habits of 15,000 miles a year, your coverage from the manufacturer expires in 28 months.”
Word track — the benefit tie-down
“The benefit here will mirror the coverage from the manufacturer for the next 8 years and 120,000 miles, giving you peace of mind until April of 2029!”

“The best thing about this is…”

Refer back to the discovery session and the purpose of the car — road trips, drives to the mountains, seeing family. The tie-down must make sense to them (WIIFM).

M/D Budget & More Info

M/D Budget shows the monthly and daily cost of the product (based on the Preferred term — also usable in the custom box). More Info opens a digital brochure from your product provider.

When they hesitate

Consult the “No” — building a custom program

The software lets you build a customized program using the C.A.R.S. method and everything you gathered along the way.

Word track — narrowing the menu
“Which product do you see the least amount of value in?”
  1. The box you drag your first product from determines the rate and term of the custom program.
  2. Dragging remaining products from the Standard program provides payment relief for your client.
  3. Click Toggle Payment to show the new monthly payment.
You can move forward with no products by clicking Next with nothing in the custom box. If anything is in the custom box, an option must be initialed. Reset the custom box by refreshing the page.
Lease Menu

Consumer Leasing Options

Present every number, every time. The lease presentation is based on payments, fees, and capitalized cost reduction.

Walk the lease details

  • Monthly base payment + sales tax on base payment
  • Acquisition fee, rebate, and trade equity applied
  • Sales tax on cap cost reduction (required by law to collect and forward)
  • Total due at signing

Click the three boxes: explain sales tax on monthly payment, sales tax on cap cost reduction, then lease end value.

Lessee responsibility → product need

Explain the residual value, then the lessee's responsibilities — excessive wear and use covers mechanical defects, missing parts and keys, damaged body or glass, chipped paint, interior rips and stains, tires under 1/8" tread, and more. This generates the need for the protections you're about to show. Click Disclosure, explain, then Next.

The three lease packages

Full Service Lease

  • Lease-End Protection · 3 years
  • Pre-Paid Maintenance · 3/36,000
  • Multi-Protect Bundle
  • Appearance Protection · 5 years

Click Full Service Lease immediately after landing on this page.

Standard Lease

  • Lease-End Protection · 3 years
  • Pre-Paid Maintenance · 3/36,000

Limited Lease

  • Vehicle Service Contract · 10/120,000
  • Windshield Protection · 3 years
Lease-End Protection helps minimize out-of-pocket costs at lease end — waiving up to $5,000 in covered excess wear and tear (exterior dents and scratches, windshield chips, interior stains, tire wear, and missing parts up to $150). Click and explain each offering, tied to their primary buying motivators.
Cash Menu

Cash deals still get the menu

The first page of the cash menu is identical to the finance menu; the second page's clickable boxes differ.

  1. Click Total Balance and discuss the dollar amount.
  2. Click Lien — if any portion of the balance is being borrowed, lien holder information must be obtained for title.
  3. Click Disclosure and proceed as you would with a finance menu.
  4. Present Preferred and Standard programs exactly like a finance deal — you can drag products to create a custom program.
You cannot drag products from the Budget box unless the deal is switched to finance — that's reserved for converting a cash deal. Click Switch to Finance, pull the term and products from Budget, then Toggle Payment to show the new monthly payment.
Post Menu

Confirm, print, and finalize

  1. Confirm the acknowledgement — the client signs that all benefits were explained and they hold the dealership harmless for any protections they refused.
  2. Print the Repayment Options page for your processor or team lead — it lists the products purchased and declined.
  3. Continue to Forms — select the electronic documents (sliders turn from gray when selected). Additional forms may be printed by your team lead or processing department.
  4. Fill in remaining data for forms like the delivery checklist; verify what processing entered.
  5. Review, sign, finalize — review each document with your client, collect signatures, then click Finalize. The deal turns dark blue in the Deals list. Team leads handle the DMS Push.
